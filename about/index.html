Our story

Foundry started when our founders — former facilities and operations leads at a Front Range manufacturer — got tired of contractors who handed over a beautiful building that was genuinely hard to actually run. So they built a general contractor around the opposite idea: understand the operation first, design the building around it second.

Today Foundry runs industrial, warehouse, and corporate projects side by side, which keeps our crews sharp across every building type your growth might require next.
