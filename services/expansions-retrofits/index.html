Services / Expansions & Retrofits

Grow the facility without stopping the line

Most expansions fail on planning, not construction — the build itself is straightforward, keeping your existing operation running through it isn't. That's the part we specialize in.

Request a Bid

What's included

  • Phasing plans built around your shift schedule and production calendar
  • Temporary utility and fire-life-safety measures for the active portion of the facility
  • Structural tie-ins engineered to minimize downtime during connection points
  • Equipment relocation and reinstallation coordination
  • Off-hours and weekend scheduling for the most disruptive work
  • Daily coordination with your operations team, not just a weekly check-in

Typical timeline

Timelines vary widely by scope — a straightforward 15,000 sq ft addition can run 6–9 months, while a full line reconfiguration with tie-ins to live utilities can take longer purely due to the sequencing required to protect uptime. We'll give you a realistic, phased schedule in your quote.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee zero downtime?

We plan for it and hit it on most projects, but we're upfront in the quote about any tie-in points that genuinely require a planned, short shutdown window — no surprises mid-project.

Do you work around our specific shift schedule?

Yes — tell us your shift pattern and we build the construction schedule around it, not the reverse.

Can you handle equipment moves as part of the project?

Yes, we coordinate directly with your equipment vendors or riggers for relocation and reinstallation as part of the overall schedule.
