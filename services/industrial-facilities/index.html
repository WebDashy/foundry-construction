Services / Industrial Facilities

Facilities engineered around your equipment, not the other way around

Manufacturing and processing space has requirements a standard shell doesn't solve — power, ventilation, floor loading, equipment clearances. We design to those specs from the first drawing.

What's included

  • Utility and power-load planning coordinated with your equipment vendors
  • Reinforced floor systems specified for your actual equipment weights
  • Ventilation and process-exhaust design for your specific operation
  • Permitting and inspections handled start to finish, including fire marshal sign-off for regulated processes
  • Phased construction if you need to keep existing lines running
  • Commissioning support through equipment install and startup

Typical timeline

A standard 40,000–80,000 sq ft facility typically runs 10–16 months from permit to certificate of occupancy, depending on utility infrastructure and equipment lead times. We build your equipment vendors' timelines into the schedule up front, not as an afterthought.

Frequently asked questions

Can you coordinate directly with our equipment vendors?

Yes — we regularly run pre-construction meetings with equipment vendors to lock in power, utility, and clearance requirements before foundations are poured.

Do you handle specialized permitting for industrial processes?

Yes, including air quality, hazardous materials, and fire code permitting specific to your process type.

Can you build while our existing facility stays operational?

For expansions and adjacent-site builds, yes — see our Expansions & Retrofits service for facilities that need to stay live during construction.
