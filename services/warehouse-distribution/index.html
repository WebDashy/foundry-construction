Services / Warehouse & Distribution

Built for throughput, not just square footage

A warehouse that looks right on paper can still bottleneck your operation if dock count, clear height, and racking layout don't match how you actually move product. We design around your fulfillment flow first.

Request a Bid

What's included

  • Dock door count and configuration planned around your carrier mix
  • Clear-height and column-spacing engineered for your racking system
  • Fire suppression (ESFR) design matched to your storage commodity class
  • Site work for trailer staging, yard circulation, and truck turning radius
  • Fixed-bid contract with vendor coordination for racking and conveyor installers
  • Certificate of occupancy timed to your go-live date, not just permit close

Typical timeline

A 100,000–200,000 sq ft distribution center typically runs 12–18 months from site work to occupancy, with racking and material-handling systems often installed in the final 8–10 weeks in parallel with building close-out.

Frequently asked questions

Can you coordinate with our racking and MHE vendors?

Yes — we build your racking, conveyor, and material-handling vendors' install windows directly into the master schedule.

Do you handle ESFR sprinkler design?

Yes, matched to your specific storage commodity classification — this gets locked early since it drives clear-height and racking decisions.

Can you build in phases if we're leasing part of the space initially?

Yes — we regularly phase distribution centers so a portion can go live while build-out continues on the remainder.
