Typical timeline

A 20,000–40,000 sq ft corporate buildout typically runs 4–7 months from permit to move-in, with occupied-floor phasing adding time but keeping your business running throughout — most clients find that trade worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Can our team stay in the building during construction?

In most cases, yes — we phase floor by floor or wing by wing and schedule noisy work outside business hours where needed.

Do you coordinate with our IT and AV vendors?

Yes — low-voltage rough-in gets scheduled alongside general construction so your IT vendor isn't fighting finished walls later.

Can you work around our lease's landlord approval process?

Yes, we regularly manage landlord/tenant improvement allowance documentation as part of the project.
